Ekua's Eatery

Ekua's Eatery is a community focused restaurant offering healthy and affordable meals.

Our Purpose:

Are you tired of unhealthy, overpriced meals? Do you want delicious food without breaking the bank? Looking for a quiet place to enjoy your meal?

We seek to provide our community with healthy delicious meals at an affordable prices. We serve a variety of dishes to suit everyone's tastes. We prioritize everyone's convenience so we have a quiet space where everyone can enjoy their meals and better we deliver to your location in no time.

Our Signature Dish:

Want to experience authentic, mouth-watering flavors? Looking for meals that combine tradition with nutrition?

For our signature dish, we serve kenkey accompanied with spicy tilapia fish and fresh vegetables, whereas for our continental dish we serve Beef Jollof with Turkey.

Color Scheme:

The website uses two primary colors to create a fresh and inviting aesthetic

  • Dark Green (rgb(24, 120, 24)): Used for the header and footer to establish a strong, professional foundation that represents health and growth.
  • Light Green (rgb(120, 255, 120)): Used for the main content area to create a welcoming, fresh background that complements the dark green and promotes readability.

Typography:

The website uses a clean, readable font stack to ensure accessibility and visual appeal

  • Primary Font: Sans-serif (used for body text)
  • Header Font: Roboto (used for headings)
